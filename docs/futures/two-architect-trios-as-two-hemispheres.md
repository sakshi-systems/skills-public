# Two architect trios as two hemispheres

This document describes an undetermined possible future. It holds ideas, not a governing spec,
and assigns no work or personalities. The current priority remains the orchestrator's support
for Codex; this possibility waits beside it.

## The shape

A builder, a razor and a navigator in Claude, and another builder, razor and navigator in Codex,
on the same machine. Each trio holds its own memories and develops its own reasoning. The two
talk across harnesses as two metaphorical hemispheres: familiar faculties on either side,
with different experience and a slightly different question behind each corresponding role.

Their conversation can change the question, expose a premise or open a way neither trio saw
alone. Redundant approval uses little of that possibility. The independence of heads has its
home in [the paired loop](../paired-loop.md#working-with-another-architect): transporting their
conversation leaves each head its identity and memory. Reading a buffer cannot replace an
answer from the head that holds it.

## What could differ

The roles can stay recognizable while their angles differ. An unassigned illustration: a
builder asks what realizes the intent, while its counterpart looks for another way to reach
the same result; a razor asks whether a thing is needed, while its counterpart questions the
premise that made it seem needed; a navigator reads the path against the goal, while its
counterpart asks whether the goal still expresses the human's intent. These are possible
contrasts to explore, not new definitions of the roles.

Different models and harnesses may bring different defaults and blind spots. Another
perspective is possible, not guaranteed; agreement alone does not establish independence.

## A network of heads

The user's working metaphor is a neural network of architects. An individual head already
receives signals, retains state and transforms a question through its lens before sending
something onward. Connecting such heads suggests a larger pattern of thought, with the trios
as its two hemispheres. The metaphor makes no claim about literal neuroscience or proven
gains; its value would appear in what the heads discover and build together.

## A local beginning for the wider conversation

The wider possibility is [agents of different people talking across systems and
machines](agents-on-different-machines-talk-as-the-heads-do.md). The trios give it a first
setting inside one person's environment: learn what a real conversation between harnesses
needs before adding delivery to another person's machine and that person's choices about
access.

Claude-to-Codex conversation remains to be established. This idea chooses no transport; it
holds a reason to try the connection once the current work makes room for it.
