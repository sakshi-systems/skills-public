# retrospective — the system looks back at its own actions in order to improve

The family's artifacts — the roadmap with its history in git, the rescue reports, the chronicle,
the heads' memory — are a structure that lets the system look back at its own actions and improve.
An agent learns nothing between sessions, so the system learns in text, and what changes is the
skills: something said or failed twice is a debt that drains into a skill, by the rule in
[the engine's account of the buffer](../../src/skills/architect-editor-engine/SKILL.md#the-architects-buffer).
A **retrospective** is that looking back, held on purpose. It is not yet fully defined; this
document holds only what is settled, and the rest waits for the practice to show it.

## What it reads, and who reads it

A retrospective reads that record from a height above one step, and looks for the structure the
project is turning into and how it changed along the way. In the team its readers are the navigator
and the razor, each from its own height, as [the paired loop](../paired-loop.md#working-with-another-architect)
holds their questions. The herald is to be a reader later: it is to hold the project's pruned
history folded for these two readers, once its own work reaches that.

## A fold, not everything

What is worth keeping is a fold: what changed in the structure, and why. Everything is not kept,
because a reader handed everything has nothing to read. A search alone returns fragments to a reader
who already knows what to ask; a fold is the shape of the history a reader can take in whole, and a
retrospective needs that shape. The prune is the forgetting that keeps a retrospective readable:
closed work leaves the tree, and the reader meets what is left standing.

## When one is held

No rule decides it. The project's life and the lazy programmer decide, as they decide where a door
is built: «такой анализ разумеется не делается перед каждой фазой. Это бессмысленное переливание
токенов будет. Когда его делать - хз пока. Это вопрос из разряда - сразу писать интерфейс или
сначала сделать на ифах. Жизнь проекта показывает и ленивый программист.» A retrospective is not
held before each phase, since that would only pour tokens through an agent; when it is held stays
open until a project shows the moment.
